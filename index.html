Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LyriFlow ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the app's translation features, advertising, external links, and support services.

For privacy questions or support, contact lyriflowapp@gmail.com .

2. Text and Translations

LyriFlow uses Google ML Kit to translate text on your device. This translation feature does not send the text you enter or the resulting translation to Google's servers for translation.

The current feature treats input text as English and translates it into the selected supported language. An internet connection is required to download necessary language models. Downloads use device storage and may use mobile data.

The current implementation does not send your translation text to a developer-operated server or provide a developer-operated translation-history account. Text may remain visible while you use the app. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

3. ML Kit Technical Information

On-device translation does not mean that the entire app operates without network communication. ML Kit communicates with Google for model downloads and may report technical and usage information.

Depending on the service and configuration, this can include device and app information, installation identifiers, performance metrics, input and output sizes, and configured translation languages. This technical information is separate from the actual text and translation results.

Learn more: ML Kit Terms and Privacy .

4. Advertising

LyriFlow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google's advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and maintain service reliability. An IP address may be used to estimate general location; this is different from requesting precise GPS location.

Advertising behavior depends on factors including your region, device settings, consent choices, and the app's advertising configuration.

5. Analytics

LyriFlow version 1.0.35 includes the Google Analytics for Firebase library, but Firebase Analytics collection is deactivated in this app version through the app's configuration.

This setting does not disable the separate data processing performed by Google AdMob, ML Kit, external websites, or email support described elsewhere in this policy.

Deactivating collection in this version does not delete information previously collected by earlier versions. Previously collected information remains subject to the relevant service's retention settings and policies.
